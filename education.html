Creating welcoming spaces for learning

The photograph shows children gathered during a Hope for Life South Sudan activity focused on learning, care and community connection.

Creating welcoming spaces for children can support participation in educational activities while giving them encouragement, attention and a safe environment to learn and interact.

For Hope for Life South Sudan, education is part of a wider pathway that connects children with dignity, protection, health and opportunities for a more secure future.

This story highlights

  • Creating welcoming spaces for children.
  • Encouraging participation in learning.
  • Providing care and positive community connection.
  • Linking education with dignity and opportunity.
