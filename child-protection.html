CHILD PROTECTION

Standing With Children Affected by Conflict

Our CEO met with children affected by conflict from Upper Nile State, listened to their experiences, and reaffirmed the organization’s commitment to supporting them and promote their safety and well-being.

Listening, supporting and protecting

The meeting provided an opportunity for Hope for Life South Sudan’s CEO to spend time with children affected by conflict from Upper Nile State and hear directly about their experiences.

The organization reaffirmed its commitment to supporting these children and to promoting their safety and well-being. The photographs on this page capture moments of engagement, care and community activity around children.

For Hope for Life South Sudan, child protection means keeping children at the center of conversations about their needs, listening to their experiences and strengthening the support around them.

This story highlights

  • Listening to children affected by conflict.
  • Supporting children and their communities.
  • Promoting safety and well-being.
  • Keeping children at the center of protection efforts.
